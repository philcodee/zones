# Session Log: Webcam Floor Plan Prototype

## 2026-10-27

### Goal
Build the prototype described in `webcam-floorplan-prototype-plan.md` (v2): a webcam above a printed 3rd floor plan, where ArUco tokens placed on rooms make bookings.

### Built (`prototype/`)
- **Browser app** (`index.html`, `app.js`, `style.css`): three modes.
  - **Simulate:** drag tokens onto rooms to try the booking rules with no camera.
  - **Camera:** live detection with room outlines drawn over the video.
  - **Projector view:** fullscreen, black background, light room colors, 4-point alignment to the paper.
- **`core.js`**: no DOM, tested in Node.
  - Homography: least-squares fit to the corner points.
  - Point-in-room lookup.
  - `BookingEngine` rules:
    - a token books a room after sitting still for 1 s;
    - a token missing for 1.5 s counts as lifted;
    - a second token on a booked room is blocked, and takes the room when the first is lifted;
    - bookings expire on their own, and the token reads "spent" until lifted;
    - if the camera stalls, bookings are kept, not ended;
    - bookings are saved in `localStorage`.
- **`detector.worker.js`**: ArUco detection runs off the main thread. The library is js-aruco2 2.0.0, saved in `vendor/`.
- **`rooms.json`**: 19 room polygons traced from the plan.
  - Bookable: 7 core rooms.
  - "Maybe" (switched on by a checkbox): 309, 312 Office, 305, 301.
  - Each room has its own internal ID because the plan's numbers repeat (two rooms are numbered 312).
  - The **Edit rooms** mode lets you drag outline corners and download an updated file.
- **`tools/generate_markers.py`**: creates the plan print, the token sheet, spare corner markers and `plan.json`.
- **README.md**: how to run, print and set up.

### Decisions
- **Dictionary:** `DICT_4X4_50` everywhere. In the browser it's the first 50 codes of js-aruco2's `ARUCO_4X4_1000`. Checked that they match markers generated by OpenCV.
- **Durations (Open Question 4):** one token per duration.
  - 10–17 = 30 min
  - 20–27 = 60 min
  - 30–37 = 90 min
- **Who booked (Open Question 5):** shows the token number only for now.
- **Group breakout (Open Question 1):** not assigned yet. The Gallery is a "maybe" room in case it's chosen.
- **Room coordinates:** source PNG pixels (3952 × 5194, portrait). The print is rotated 90° clockwise to landscape.

### Changes after testing on real hardware
1. **Corners flickered and the room outlines were warped.**
   - **Cause:** the detector allowed near matches across 1,000 codes. A plan detail was misread as a corner ID, which distorted the mapping.
   - **Fixes:**
     - match against only the 50 printed codes, allowing at most 1 wrong bit;
     - calibrate only from markers read exactly;
     - keep one reading per ID;
     - reject fits where the points don't agree;
     - calibrate from any 3 corners.
2. **The print is 11×17, not the 24×18 the plan assumed.**
   - The plan now prints as an exact 17 × 11 in page, at 402 dpi, with a 0.25 in safe margin.
   - Corner markers (1.4 in) moved to the left and right ends to use the sheet's width. The plan is about 20% larger than on the old print.
   - Tokens reduced from 1 in to 0.75 in. The Phone Booth is about 0.7 in tall at this scale.
   - Token size on screen now comes from `plan.json`.
   - **The old print no longer matches `plan.json`. Reprint the plan and the tokens.**
3. **Calibration still jumpy.**
   - **Cause:** the mapping was rebuilt from scratch on every frame.
   - **Fix:** calibration is now held.
     - Corner positions are smoothed over time.
     - A covered corner keeps its last position.
     - A corner re-snaps only after about 6 consistent frames somewhere new (a real bump).
     - A **Recalibrate** button forces a fresh start.
     - In a simulation, outline jitter dropped from about 2.5 px to about 0.6 px.
   - The status line now shows the camera resolution.

### Tests (synthetic camera frames: tilted, blurred, rendered from the real print)
- 24×18 layout at 1080p: all markers found, corner fit error 1.2 px, 5 of 5 tokens in the correct room.
- 11×17 layout, all 4 corners in view:

  | Resolution | Corners | Tokens correct | Detection time |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1080p | 4 of 4 | 6 of 6 | 287 ms |
  | 720p | 4 of 4 | 6 of 6 | 46 ms |

- With one corner covered, 3-corner calibration still placed every token correctly.
- The booking engine passed tests for double booking, handoff, lifting, expiry, a camera stall, a non-bookable room and the "maybe" toggle.
- The UI was not tested in a browser. It was checked only on real hardware, by the user.

### Observations from hardware
- The camera is probably 1280×720, judging by the 20 fps. On the old shrunk print:
  - corner markers were about 70 px across, which is fine;
  - tokens were about 40 px, which is borderline.
- The top-right corner marker was cropped by the frame edge. Markers need visible white paper around them to be read.
