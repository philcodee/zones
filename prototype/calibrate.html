What to aim for

  • 3–5 px per square at the detector (the "det" number). Each tag is a 6 × 6 grid. This detector loses tag outlines when squares are much bigger than that, so leave Detect at on Auto, which shrinks the frame to fit. The camera's own px per square just needs to be at least 3.
  • Read rate ≥ 95 % and exact ≥ 90 %. The booking app only calibrates from exact reads.
  • Jitter < 1 px once focus is locked. Higher means blur, noise or auto-exposure hunting.
  • Fit error < 0.1″ with all four tags: how far the detected tag corners are from one flat-sheet fit, measured on the paper. One corner much worse than the rest means a misread; everything a bit high means lens distortion or a sheet that isn't flat.
  • Fill the frame with the sheet, avoid glare on the tags, and lock focus once it's sharp.
