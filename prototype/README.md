# Webcam Floor Plan — Prototype

Builds Phases 1–7 of `../webcam-floorplan-prototype-plan.md`.

## Run it

```sh
cd ~/Desktop/zones/prototype
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Open http://localhost:8000. The camera only works over `localhost` or HTTPS.

**Simulate** (default) lets you try the booking rules with no camera: drag tokens onto rooms.
**Camera** runs the real pipeline.

## Print

`python3 tools/generate_markers.py` writes the following to `print/`:

| File | What | Print at |
|---|---|---|
| `plan_print.pdf` | Plan rotated to landscape, in light gray, with 1.4 in corner markers 0–3 at the left and right ends | 11 × 17 landscape, **actual size / 100%**, matte |
| `tokens.pdf` | 24 booking tokens, 0.75 in each, with cut lines | Letter, 100% |
| `corners.pdf` | Spare corner markers | Letter |

Re-running the script also rewrites `plan.json`, which tells the browser where the corner markers sit.
If you print at a different size, keep the proportions; the homography handles scale.

## Tokens

One token per duration (answers Open Question 4 for now):

| IDs | Duration |
|---|---|
| 10–17 | 30 min |
| 20–27 | 60 min |
| 30–37 | 90 min |

All markers use `DICT_4X4_50`. In the browser, `vendor/` is js-aruco2 2.0.0. Its `ARUCO_4X4_1000`
dictionary starts with the same 50 codes; this was checked against markers generated by OpenCV.

## Booking rules (`core.js`)

- A token must stay still in one room for **1 s** before it counts.
- A token missing for **1.5 s** counts as lifted, and its booking ends.
- One booking per room. A second token on a booked room gets "Room taken" and waits.
  If the first token is lifted, the waiting token takes the room.
- When a booking runs out, the room becomes available again. The token shows "Expired · lift"
  and won't book again until someone lifts it and puts it back.
- If the camera stops or loses calibration, bookings are kept, not ended.
- Bookings are saved in `localStorage` and survive a reload.

## Rooms (`rooms.json`)

Polygons are in pixels of `assets/floor-plan.png` (3952 × 5194, portrait).
`bookable` is `true`, `false`, or `"maybe"`. "Maybe" rooms are Offices 309 and 312, Lounge 305, and Gallery 301.
The **Include "maybe" rooms** checkbox turns them on.

To adjust the outlines, use **Edit rooms**, drag the corner dots, then **Download rooms.json** and replace the file.
Two rooms are both numbered 312 (Women and Office), so each room uses an internal ID (`r312w`, `r312o`).

## Camera setup

1. Mount the webcam overhead and frame the whole sheet, including all 4 corner markers.
2. Choose **Camera**, then **Start**. Yellow outlines are corner markers and blue outlines are tokens.
   Once all 4 corners are seen, the room outlines appear over the video. Check that they match the paper.
3. Press **Lock** to fix focus and exposure if the camera allows it. Many built-in webcams don't.
4. If a corner is briefly covered, the app keeps the last calibration ("last fix").

## Projector

Click **Projector view**. It goes fullscreen with a black background and shows only light room colors and labels.
Press **A** and drag the 4 dots onto the 4 corners of the printed plan. Press **R** to reset and **Esc** to exit.
Use **Rotate 90°** if the map's orientation doesn't match the table.
