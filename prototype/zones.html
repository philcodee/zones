      Said: —

      Tones: rising = entered a zone, falling = left a zone, single high = placed, low = removed, double blip = next to each other / apart, short tick = next to / away from a zone. Pointer ticks: a steady tick while a pointer tag aims at a zone or the outer wall — faster = closer; each pointer has its own tone. Press S anytime to hear what’s where, Q to stop talking.

      Mixer

      Changes apply right away and are remembered in this browser. To make them the defaults for everyone, copy or save the settings into prototype/zones.json. That includes the mixer and the Rules settings below.

      Events

        Rules

        Distances are on the paper, measured centre to centre for tokens, and from a token’s centre to the zone outline for “next to” a zone. A change counts once it has held for 0.3 s. A covered token keeps its place (shown faded on the map) until it has been hidden longer than the removal time; raise that if hands covering tags trigger “removed”. Pointer distance is from the pointer tag’s front edge to where the beam first meets a zone edge or the outer wall (dashed yellow on the map). Zone edges stop the beam from inside as well as outside.
